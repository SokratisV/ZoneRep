# ZoneRep

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-15)

First release.

- Reputation per zone and faction from Questie's quest data: total, earned, left to earn, with a progress
  bar; zone and overall totals.
- By zone / by faction views, current-zone jump, expand and collapse all, hide finished.
- Search by zone, faction or quest name.
- Click a faction row to list its remaining quests; click a quest to mark its quest giver (or turn-in) on
  the world map with a pin; right-click a pin to remove it; pins clear when the quest is picked up or handed in.
- Rep from kills and other sources, tracked per zone and character from install.
- Faction names from Questie's list when the client has none.
- FlareUI-style window.
